Methodology

The ranking is intentionally conversion-heavy to distinguish implementation activity from mature execution.

Overall score = 30% Coverage + 70% Conversion.
Coverage = (In Deployment + Technical Go-Live + Go-Live + Live with Savings) / In Scope.
Conversion = (Technical Go-Live + Go-Live + Live with Savings) / In Scope.

Initiative priority weights are taken directly from the ranking source model.
CEO action: Review the ROI of Fab.Operate in every region. An implementation should only be considered successful when adoption and the expected productivity/cost benefits are validated.

Executive insights

The leadership race changed

NCA now ranks #1, with MAR and SouthCone close behind. The three leaders are separated by only 2.1 score points, so execution quality—not pipeline volume—will determine the next move.

Bottom regions require different interventions

BRA needs a broad recovery across high-priority initiatives; CSC needs conversion in Training/QA/HAIrEX; US-Canada needs approvals and value realization on top of an already mature Centrical/Datanitiv base.

EMA QA remains the clearest portfolio gap

No region has more than 3.8% conversion in EMA QA. Client approval, security reviews, data ingestion and calibration remain the recurring constraints.

Go-Live is not enough

Leadership comments repeatedly call out adoption, KPI quality, savings validation and manual work. The transformation should be governed against verified productivity/cost benefits, not deployment status alone.

Coverage vs. conversion

The upper-right reflects regions combining scale and conversion; the lower-left identifies where implementation breadth is not yet translating into mature outcomes.

Strategic regional readout

The bottom three are expanded by default; all regions remain available for context.

NCA · #159.79 score
Strategic reading

NCA leads the ranking, but WFM and QA remain the main threats to sustaining the position.

Why

Strong execution in Product Training, Onboarding, Pre-process, Hallo and HAIrEX lifts the portfolio. Datanitiv conversion is only 19.5%; the CEO Status shows parts of the region still on hold because BMS does not yet eliminate manual roster work and license economics remain a concern. EMA QA conversion is 3.8%. OXXO remains below its call-ingestion target (~5% vs. 20%), limiting production readiness.

Leadership signal

Supervisors reports a broad Centrical pipeline with adoption and savings validation still required after Go-Live. The biggest risk is allowing strong deployment counts to move ahead of KPI/data quality and verified benefits.

CEO action

Close the BMS decision, stabilize OXXO ingestion, and require benefit validation for the Centrical/Training accounts already live before expanding the next wave.

MAR · #259.02 score
Strategic reading

MAR remains #2 with broad execution, but several mature pipelines still need to convert into stable, measurable benefits.

Why

Centrical, HAIrEX, Hallo and RTM are strong and provide a good execution base. Product Training conversion is 22.9% and EMA QA conversion is 3.6%, showing that approvals and execution are still constraining two important value pools. Datanitiv conversion is 38.2%; WFM reports persistent technical issues, daily refresh needs and unannounced format changes.

Leadership signal

Training has 18 accounts in approval stages. QA is at 45% overall progress with one account live. Supervisors flags KPI quality issues that can undermine trust in Centrical if data is not stabilized.

CEO action

Accelerate approvals, enforce KPI/data-quality gates, and move the conversation from implementation status to adoption and verified savings for mature accounts.

SouthCone · #357.68 score

Why

Leadership signal

QA still depends on client/security approval. Argentina continues to use Excel in parallel with Datanitiv, and RTM BOT is blocked by Power Apps/Power Automate licensing. Supervisors reports unresolved global incidents and product-prioritization issues.

CEO action

Resolve the EMA approval path, decide the RTM licensing approach, eliminate parallel Excel/Datanitiv work, and define whether HAIrEX has enough demand to restart.
